## Prompt พื้นฐาน
```
Delicate pastel watercolor illustration on cold-press watercolor paper: soft wet-on-wet washes, gentle color bleeds and blooms, thin loose brown ink outlines, lots of white paper showing. Palette: peach, dusty pink, mint, soft sky blue, butter yellow. Simple faceless human figures with smooth round blank heads, warm gentle body language. Keep the top 40% of the image mostly empty white paper with only a faint wash (space for speech bubbles added later). No text, no letters, no speech bubbles, no signature.
```
