## Prompt พื้นฐาน
```
Minimalist continuous single-line drawing: one unbroken thin black ink line forming the figures and objects, elegant and loose, on a flat warm cream background. One small accent shape filled with terracotta orange. No shading, huge negative space. Simple faceless human figures with round blank heads. Keep the top 40% of the image empty cream background (space for speech bubbles added later). No text, no letters, no speech bubbles, no signature.
```
