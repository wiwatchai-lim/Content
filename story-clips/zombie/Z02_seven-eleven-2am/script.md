- **Series:** กรุงเทพฯ ไม่มีพรุ่งนี้ (see ../SERIES.md). Pure zombie action, no eyewear / shop / brand content.

- **Light:** only เต้'s phone torch and the blue moonlight through the glass; emergency-exit sign glowing green.
- **Voice:** one narrator voice (Gemini TTS, Sulafat); character lines in quotes with the name in the subtitle.
- **On screen at once:** max 2 heroes + 3 runners; outside the glass the crowd is silhouettes with white eye dots.
- **Props carried over from Z01:** เต้'s half-face helmet, ฝน's IV pole, the scratch on ฝน's left forearm (taped with gauze).

## Fight choreography (store, 22.0–62.0 = 40 s)
1. **Breach (22.0):** three runners hit the glass at full sprint; the pane shatters; runner 1 slides across the floor on the glass.
2. **Shelf barricade (25.0):** ฝน kicks the end of the snack aisle; the whole shelf topples across the entrance like a domino. `โครม!` Runner 1 is pinned under it, arms flailing.
3. **Freezer-door clamp (28.0):** runner 2 climbs over the fallen shelf and runs along the drink coolers; เต้ yanks a cooler door open in its path, it runs face-first into the glass door, then เต้ slams the door on its neck. `เคร้ง!` Frost puffs out.
4. **Canned-fish barrage (31.0):** runner 2 pulls free; เต้ grabs cans from the shelf and throws three fast — two miss (one dents the ATM, `ป๊อก!`), the third hits square in the forehead. `โป๊ก!`
5. **Funny line (34.0):** เต้ shouts about collecting stamps for 300 baht while ducking a swipe; ฝน rolls her eyes and swings the IV pole into runner 2's shins to drop it, then into its head.
6. **Ice-cream trap (37.0–46.0):** ฝน yells to open the chest freezer; เต้ flips the lid up. Runner 3 sprints at ฝน; she sidesteps at the last instant like a bullfighter, hooking its ankle with the IV pole; it dives head-first into the freezer among ice-cream boxes. เต้ slams the lid, ฝน sits on it; thumping from inside lifts her a little each time. `ปึ้ง! ปึ้ง!` เต้ stacks drink crates on top.
7. **Slow-mo counter leap (46.0–53.0):** runner 1, freed from the shelf, the biggest one, launches over the counter. Slow-mo: snack bags floating, its jaw wide; เต้ spins the helmet by the strap, full arc. Back to speed: `ตุ้บ!` It crashes into the cigarette rack.
8. **Near-miss (53.0):** a fourth runner crawls under the fallen shelf toward เต้'s ankle, unseen; its hand is an inch from his leg.
9. **The bark (56.0):** a dog barks behind the stock-room door. เต้ turns at the sound, sees the crawler, and drops a Muay Thai downward elbow onto its head. It stays down.
10. **Seal (62.0):** they push the tall cooler over the broken front; the outside runners lose the noise and drift off.
