# Z05 — ลุงชาญ

Series: กรุงเทพฯ ไม่มีพรุ่งนี้ (see ../SERIES.md). Thai VO + Thai subtitles, notes in English.
Picks up Z04's cliffhanger: Mew was bitten on the 9th floor… and did not turn.

- **Logline:** Hiding in a dead mall's car park while they wait to see if Mew turns, the group is hunted by a brute built out of car parts. An old security guard with a Krabong staff steps out of the dark and saves them, and his radio brings the only plan they have.
- **Length:** ~90 s (cold open 0–3, build 3–20, fight 20–60, breather 60–76, cliffhanger 76–90)
- **Clock:** Day 2, 15:40 → 15:47 (daylight outside; walkers slow, hunt by sound). Clock chip top-left.
- **Location:** B2 car park of an abandoned mall near the hospital, ย่านพญาไท. Concrete pillars painted with bay numbers (B2-14…), dead cars, one flickering tube light, ramp up to daylight, a security booth with a boom barrier.
- **On screen at once (max):** Tae, Fon, Mew, Khao Tang, Lung Chan + 1 brute + 2 walkers.

## Character silhouettes (for SVG)
- **เต้** — orange win vest, full-face helmet carried in hand, wrapped fists.
- **ฝน** — teal scrubs, hair bun, aluminium IV pole (wheels removed, hook end).
- **มิว** — yellow T-shirt, bandage on left forearm (bite from Z04), small backpack.
- **ข้าวตัง** — small brown dog, curly tail, red collar.
- **ลุงชาญ** — navy security uniform, cap, grey moustache, rattan Krabong staff (ไม้พลอง) with red tape at both ends, whistle on a cord, handheld radio on the belt.
- **Brute (ตัวยักษ์)** — 2× human height, hunched; car door bolted on its chest as a shield, a tyre around one shoulder, a traffic cone stuck on one fist, exposed head (weak point).

## Fight choreography (0:26–1:00, 34 s + 0:20–0:26 approach = ≥ 35 s set-piece)
1. **0:26** Brute swings the cone fist sideways. Tae ducks; the fist smashes a car windscreen behind him. `เพล้ง!`
2. **0:27** Tae jumps on the car's bonnet, leaps, swings the helmet at the brute's head — only reaches the car-door chest. `เคร้ง!` Helmet bounces back into Tae's own face. Tae: "โอ๊ย… ตีตัวเอง" (small joke sub, white).
3. **0:29** Brute backhands. Tae rolls off the bonnet; the tyre on the brute's shoulder skims his hair. Near-miss whoosh lines.
4. **0:31** Two walkers shuffle in from behind pillar B2-16 toward Mew. Khao Tang bites one's ankle and pulls; it trips into the other. `ตุ้บ!`
5. **0:32** Fon spins the IV pole, hook end hooks the first walker's neck, pulls it down, heel of the pole to the head. `ป๊อก!` Second one: pole jab to the forehead. Drop.
6. **0:34** Fon yells line 10 ("ขามัน! เอ็นร้อยหวาย!"). Tae slides under the brute's legs on his knees (sparks from his kneepads), elbow to the back of the brute's ankle. `ตุ้บ!`
7. **0:36** Brute drops to one knee — the floor cracks — but swings round and grabs Tae by the vest.
8. **0:38** Brute lifts Tae high, Tae kicks in the air: "ปล่อย! ผมยังผ่อนรถไม่หมด!" (funny line, yellow mini sub).
9. **0:40** Brute slams Tae onto the bonnet of a pickup. `โครม!` Car alarm starts screaming. Red danger tag: `เสียงดัง = ฝูงมา` (rule reminder).
10. **0:43** Whistle. Lung Chan steps out (line 12–13).
11. **0:47** Lung Chan's staff: one strike to the wrist holding Tae (`ป๊อก!`) — brute lets go — Tae drops and rolls clear. Second strike: tip into the car's alarm module through the grille, the alarm dies. Silence.
12. **0:50** Krabong sequence, rhythmic, each hit on a beat: staff sweeps the brute's good knee (`ตุ้บ`), staff butt up under the chin (`ปั้ก`), spin, staff wedged in the gap behind the car-door armour, Lung Chan levers — the door pops loose and clatters away. `เคร้ง!`
13. **0:54 SLOW-MO** (all motion ×0.25, colour drained except Tae's orange vest): brute falls forward toward Lung Chan, mouth open. Lung Chan plants the staff on the floor as a pole-vault; Tae runs, steps on Lung Chan's bent knee, launches upward, helmet held in both hands above his head. Dust motes hang in the light. Line 15 plays over it.
14. **0:57** Speed snaps back: helmet smashes down on the brute's bare head. `ตุ้บ!!` Brute goes face-down. `โครม!!` The cone pops off its fist and rolls to Mew's feet. Mew puts it on Khao Tang's head. Beat of silence.
