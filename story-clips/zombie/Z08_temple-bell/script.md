# Z08 — ระฆังวัด

- **Series:** กรุงเทพฯ ไม่มีพรุ่งนี้ (EP 8/10) — emotional peak
- **Logline:** เสียงเห่าของข้าวตังพาทุกคนมาถึงวัดริมคลอง แต่ไฟจากจตุจักรลากฝูงซอมบี้ตามมาล้อมกำแพง ลุงชาญถูกกัดระหว่างปกป้องมิว และเลือกใช้นาทีสุดท้ายตีระฆังเรียกฝูงไปหาตัวเอง ให้เด็ก ๆ หนีลงเรือ
- **Length:** ~90 s, vertical 9:16
- **Clock:** 00:41 → 00:53 Day 3 (on-screen clock chip; ship leaves at dawn)
- **Location:** a small old temple beside a canal off the Chao Phraya, near Chatuchak — low white wall, wooden main gate, bell tower (หอระฆัง) on a brick base, back gate to a canal pier with a long-tail boat
- **On screen:** max 3 humans + dog + 3–4 zombie silhouettes on the wall at once. Cast: เต้, ฝน, มิว, ลุงชาญ, ข้าวตัง
- **Picks up Z07:** Khao Tang fell into the dark during the roof jump. **How he returns:** he ran ahead by smell toward the canal and is standing on the temple wall barking; his barking leads the group to the temple.
- **Rules used:** walkers + runners (night), bite turns in 1–3 min (except Mew), head hit drops them, loud noise pulls hordes.

## 3. Fight choreography (0:22–0:56, 34 s + gate aftermath to 1:00 ≈ 38 s)

1. **0:22** Walkers pile against the wall; the ones behind climb over their backs — a ramp of bodies. Heads pop above the wall top.
2. **0:24** Lung Chan on the wall walk: staff jab, jab, sweep — three heads drop back one after another. `ป๊อก! ป๊อก! ฟาด!`
3. **0:27** Tae kicks a climbing walker in the face with a Muay Thai teep; it falls and knocks the two below it down like dominoes. `ตุ้บ! ตุ้บ! ตุ้บ!`
4. **0:28** Fon: "ฝั่งซ้าย! ตัววิ่ง!" — a runner sprints up the body ramp and leaps the wall.
5. **0:30** Fon meets it mid-air with the IV pole across the chest, it lands on its back on the courtyard tiles; she finishes it with a downward strike. `เคร้ง! ตุ้บ!`
6. **0:32** Two hands grab Tae's ankle; he's pulled half over the wall, hanging upside down over the horde (near-miss, teeth inches from his face).
7. **0:34** Tae, upside down: "บุญยังไม่ทันทำ ต้องมาต่อยในวัดอีก!" — swings the helmet down: `เคร้ง!` Lung Chan hauls him back by the belt. `ฮึบ!`
8. **0:37** Khao Tang barks at the side wall: a runner is coming over the far corner toward Mew. Khao Tang charges and bites its trouser leg, it trips. Fon finishes it. `ตุ้บ!` (Khao Tang's save)
9. **0:40** The wooden gate buckles inward, the bar cracks. `แคร่ก… โครม!` The gate bursts; walkers stumble into the courtyard.
10. **0:42** Lung Chan jumps down from the wall into the gap, Krabong spinning in a figure-eight — four zombies dropped in a circle. `วืดวืดวืด — ฟาด!`
11. **0:45** **Slow-mo:** a runner dives at Mew from the gate shadow. Lung Chan steps between them; his forearm goes up — the runner bites into it. Mew screams "ลุง!!" (sound drops out except heartbeat).
12. **0:47** Speed back: Lung Chan headbutts the runner, staff to its skull. `โครม!` Tae + Fon drag a temple cart against the broken gate. `ครืดดด… ปึง!`
13. **0:50** Mew grabs his arm; he pulls his sleeve down fast and smiles: "แค่ถลอก". Tae notices the sleeve — beat of doubt on Tae's face.
14. **0:52** Walls held for the moment, but the cart shudders with each push; hands reach through the gaps. Fon: back gate!

## 4. Sound notes (no music)

- Ambience: crickets cut out when the horde arrives; constant low moan outside the wall; canal water lapping at the pier; a temple dog-bell chime in the wind early on.
- Hits: staff = sharp wood crack; IV pole = metal clang; helmet = big clang; teep/knees = dull thump; gate = heavy splintering wood.
- Slow-mo bite: all sound sucked out → heartbeat + Mew's scream stretched long.
- Breather: only Lung Chan's breathing, slowly roughening; the gate thumping off-screen.
- The bell: real deep temple bell, long decay. Rings get slightly slower and heavier each time (he is turning). After the last ring, cut ALL sound for 2 s — then just water against the boat.
- Leave the lines 14–18 dry and close-miked; no SFX over the goodbye.

## 5. Cliffhanger + next-episode card

From the long-tail boat drifting down the canal, Tae, Fon, Mew and Khao Tang watch the bell tower. The whole horde has turned away from them and climbs toward the tower. Lung Chan, between strikes, looks down at the boat and smiles. One more ring. The striker swings again, the bell sways, and there is no sound. Mew grips the whistle. Hold on the silent tower against the dark sky.
