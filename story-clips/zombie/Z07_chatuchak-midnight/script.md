# Z07 — จตุจักรเที่ยงคืน

- **Series:** กรุงเทพฯ ไม่มีพรุ่งนี้ (EP 7/10)
- **Logline:** หนีออกจากห้างของเสี่ยเปาได้ แต่รถน้ำมันหมดหน้าจตุจักร ทั้งห้าต้องตัดผ่านเขาวงกตแผงลอยตอนเที่ยงคืน ที่ตัววิ่งล่าตามเสียง จนต้องจุดไฟและวิ่งบนหลังคาแผงหนี แล้วข้าวตังก็หายไป
- **Length:** ~90 s, vertical 9:16
- **Clock:** 23:52 night of Day 2 → 00:06 Day 3 (on-screen clock chip top-left; runners sprint because it is night)
- **Location:** ตลาดนัดจตุจักร — narrow alleys between shuttered stalls, corrugated tin roofs, the clock tower in the centre, Kamphaeng Phet road at the edge
- **On screen:** max 3 humans + dog + 2–3 zombies at once. Cast: เต้ (helmet), ฝน (IV pole), ลุงชาญ (กระบอง staff), มิว (red hoodie, bandaged arm), ข้าวตัง (small brown dog)
- **Picks up Z06:** เสี่ยเปา was bitten, laughed and vanished in his mall. The group escaped in a stolen pickup.
- **Rules used:** runners sprint at night and hunt by sound; head hit drops them; fire + loud noise pull hordes.

## 3. Fight choreography (0:24–0:58, 34 s + roof chase to 1:16 = 52 s total action)

1. **0:24** Runner 1 sprints down the alley on all fours. Fon shoves Mew + dog behind a noodle cart. `ตึกตึกตึก!`
2. **0:26** Lung Chan plants his feet, staff sweeps low — Runner 1's legs fly out, it face-plants. `ฟาด!`
3. **0:28** Fon steps in, IV pole to the head. "ตีที่หัว!" Down. `ตุ้บ!`
4. **0:30** Runner 2 drops from a stall roof onto Tae. Tae rolls, the zombie's teeth snap where his neck was (near-miss, freeze 4 frames). `งับ!`
5. **0:32** Tae up, double elbow, then swings his helmet by the strap into its temple. `เคร้ง!`
6. **0:34** Tae kisses the helmet: "หมวกกันน็อก ใบละสามร้อย คุ้มมาก!" (comic beat, sweat-drop icon)
7. **0:37** Runner 3 lunges at Lung Chan from behind; Khao Tang barks → Lung Chan spins.
8. **0:40** "ก้มหัว!" — Tae ducks, Lung Chan's staff whistles over Tae's head and cracks Runner 3. `วืด… ป๊อก!`
9. **0:42** Tae knee-strikes the falling body away (Muay Thai knee). `ตุ้บ!`
10. **0:45** Pull back: the whole far alley fills with running silhouettes, eyes as white dots. `ตึกตึกตึกตึก`
11. **0:49** Fon spots a stall of kerosene lamps; Tae throws one on a pile of clothes racks between them and the horde. "จุด!" `ฟู่ม!`
12. **0:54** Fire wall rises; first runners skid and burn back; but every dot in the market turns toward the light and noise. Lung Chan: up!
13. **0:58** Lung Chan boosts Mew onto a stall roof with his staff as a step, Fon climbs, Tae passes the dog up, climbs last — a runner grabs his ankle, he kicks its face. `ตุ้บ!`
14. **1:03** Running on hot tin, roofs bending; Tae's roti joke.
15. **1:07** A runner climbs onto the roof behind Tae. Mew: "ข้างหลัง!" Fon spins the IV pole, hooks its neck, flips it off the roof. `โครม!`
16. **1:10** A roof panel collapses under Lung Chan; he catches the edge with the staff across two beams and swings back up.
17. **1:12** **Slow-mo:** the big gap. Tae carries Mew, leaps; Mew's hand opens, the leash slips; Khao Tang's small shape drops into the dark between stalls (blink-and-miss, viewer can catch it). Land. `ตึง!`

## 4. Sound notes (no music)

- Ambience: wind through tin roofs, distant car alarm, far-off horde moan rising after the fire.
- Runner feet: fast slaps on concrete; on tin roofs a metallic `กราว` per step.
- Hits: wood staff = dry crack; IV pole = hollow metal clang; helmet = big clang with ring; knees/elbows = dull thump.
- Fire: whoosh ignition, steady crackle under the roof chase, glass lamps popping.
- Slow-mo jump: everything drops to a low hum + heartbeat, Mew's gasp stretched.
- Breather: only panting + crackle far away. After "ข้าวตัง!" leave 1.5 s of silence — no bark.
