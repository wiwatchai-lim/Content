- **Series:** กรุงเทพฯ ไม่มีพรุ่งนี้ (see ../SERIES.md). Pure zombie action, no eyewear/shop/brand content.
- **Logline:** With Mew and Khao Tang now in the group, Tae and Fon climb onto a BTS train stuck on the viaduct since the outbreak. They fight carriage by carriage through turned passengers to reach the hospital side, then find the street below is a sea of the dead.
- **Length:** ~90 s (vertical 9:16, Thai VO + Thai subtitles).
- **Clock:** Day 2, 02:31 → 02:33 (night = runners sprint). Clock chip top-left.
- **Location:** BTS Sukhumvit line viaduct between อนุสาวรีย์ชัยฯ and พญาไท; one stuck 4-car train, lights flickering on emergency power.
- **Picks up:** Z02 cliffhanger: Mew and Khao Tang were hiding in the 7-Eleven stock room; Fon's scratch was glass, not a bite.
- **On screen at once:** max 3 heroes + 3 zombies. Mew stays behind Fon with Khao Tang in her arms during the fight.

## 3. Fight choreography (0:27–1:07, 40 s)

Carriage drawn as a side-section: seats = rounded rectangles, grab poles = vertical lines, hanging straps = small loops. Zombies = grey silhouettes with white eyes; runners lean forward.

1. **0:27** A seated zombie lunges; Fon jams the IV pole's wheeled base into its chest and pins it to the window. **"เคร้ง!"**
2. **0:29** Tae steps in, helmet in his right hand, swings it into the zombie's temple. It drops. **"ตุ้บ!"** Fon: "ตีหัว! ที่อื่นไม่หยุด!"
3. **0:32** Two more stand up from the long bench. Tae grabs the overhead bar, swings both legs up and kicks one back into the seat. **"โครม!"**
4. **0:35** The second grabs Tae's ankle while he hangs; he drops and lands an elbow on top of its head. **"ป้าก!"** Tae, to the old zombie he just knocked off the priority seat: "ขอโทษพี่ ไม่ได้ลุกให้คนแก่!"
5. **0:39** Connecting door to car 2: a runner sprints down the aisle at full speed. Fon shoves Mew + Khao Tang onto a seat behind her.
6. **0:41** Fon plants the IV pole across the doorway like a bar; the runner hits it chest-first and flips over it. **"เปรี้ยง!"**
7. **0:43** Tae finishes it with a knee to the head as it gets up. **"ตุ้บ!"** VO: "ตู้สอง… ยังมีอีก"
8. **0:45** Car 2: three zombies in a row, straps swinging. Tae hooks a strap loop round one zombie's neck and spins it into the next; both fall like dominoes. **"โครม! โครม!"**
9. **0:48** The third lunges at Fon from behind a pole. Near miss: its teeth snap shut on the pole, not her hand. **"กึก!"**
10. **0:49.6** Fon: "ก้มลง!" Tae ducks; Fon swings the IV pole flat over his head, hook end into the zombie's temple. **"เคร้ง!"**
11. **0:51–0:54 SLOW-MO:** a runner vaults over the seat backs toward Mew. Everything slows. Khao Tang leaps out of Mew's arms and bites the runner's trouser leg, pulling it off balance mid-air. Tae spins, helmet swing at full stretch: contact. Speed snaps back. **"ปั้ก!!"** (Khao Tang's save of the series.)
12. **0:55** Tae lands on his back, pats his pocket, pulls out his transit card snapped in half (plain card shape, no logo): "บัตรรถไฟฟ้าหักอีก ซวยซ้ำ!"
13. **0:58** The doors to car 3 slide open by themselves on emergency power: five silhouettes inside. No way through.
14. **1:00** Fon spots the red emergency lever by the side door. Tae holds the connecting door shut with his back as hands punch through the gap. **"ปึ้ง! ปึ้ง!"**
15. **1:01** Fon pulls the lever; the door pops outward. **"แกร๊ก!"** VO: "ประตูฉุกเฉิน… ดึงคันโยก!"
16. **1:03** Tae lets go; the zombies spill in and trip over the bodies from beat 8 (setup pays off). **"ตุ้บ ตุ้บ ตุ้บ"**
17. **1:05** Tae: "โดด!" All jump out onto the track bed (into the 1:07 slow-mo landing).
