# Z09 — เจ้าพระยา

- **Series:** กรุงเทพฯ ไม่มีพรุ่งนี้ (see ../SERIES.md). Pure zombie action, no eyewear / shop / brand content.
- **Logline:** The temple bell has stopped. Lung Chan is gone. On the stolen long-tail, Tae, Fon, Mew and Khao Tang race out of the canal into the dark Chao Phraya, but the engine noise wakes the runners on Memorial Bridge, and they start dropping onto the boat.
- **Length:** ~92 s, 9:16, Thai VO + Thai subtitles.
- **Clock:** Day 3, 01:02 → 05:52 (night, so the zombies are **runners**). On-screen clock chip top-left. After the fight Fon cuts the engine (noise pulls hordes), and they drift on the ebb tide for four hours; the clock jumps during the montage.
- **Location:** the canal behind the temple's back gate (Thonburi side) → out into the Chao Phraya → under สะพานพุทธ (Memorial Bridge) → drifting montage past สะพานตากสิน and สะพานภูมิพล → the pier at ป้อมพระจุลจอมเกล้า, ship lights in the distance.
- **Follows Z08:** the group escaped on a long-tail from the canal pier behind the back gate. From the boat they saw Lung Chan's last smile, then the bell stopped. Z09 starts seconds later on the same boat in the canal. The horde is turning back toward the sound of the engine. Mew wears Lung Chan's security whistle on its lanyard (first seen in Z05, given to her in Z08).
- **Cast on screen:** เต้ (helmet, red riding vest), ฝน (scrubs, IV pole), มิว (yellow raincoat, bandage on her **left** forearm from the Z04 bite, Lung Chan's whistle on a lanyard), ข้าวตัง (small brown dog), runners (grey-green silhouettes, ragged outlines). Never more than 3 zombies on screen at once.

## Fight choreography (00:33–01:08, 35 s)

1. **00:33 — ตุ้บ!** Runner #1 lands on the bow, crouched like a frog, one metre from Mew. Tae swings his helmet by the chin strap like a mace: **พลั่ก!** Runner #1 spins off the bow. Tae: "ลงผิดป้ายแล้วพี่!"
2. **00:36** Runner #2 lands on the roof canopy. The canvas sags with a dent shaped like a body. Fon jams the IV pole up through the canvas: **ปึ้ก!** The dent goes flat.
3. **00:38** Two more drop at once, one on each side. The boat rocks hard left and right. Water slops over the side. "ตัวที่สอง สาม สี่…"
4. **00:40** Runner #3 swipes at Tae. Tae ducks, and the claw passes so close it knocks his helmet off his head. Near-miss: the helmet rolls toward the edge, and Tae stops it with his foot.
5. **00:42** Tae flicks the helmet up into his hand and answers with an elbow to the temple: **ปั้ก!** Then a knee as it folds: **ตุ้บ!** Runner #3 goes overboard. "ฝนตกหนักเลย… ไม่ได้หมายถึงเธอนะ!" Fon shakes her head without looking at him.
6. **00:46** Runner #4 grabs Fon's IV pole from behind and they wrestle over it. Fon lets go suddenly. The runner stumbles backward into the spinning propeller shaft: **ครืดดด!** Spray goes everywhere, water only, no gore.
7. **00:48** Fon picks the pole back up, calm: "ตีที่หัว! อย่าให้มันกัด!" She swings it and shatters a hanging lantern against the next runner's head: **เคร้ง!**
8. **00:50** The tiller is left alone. The boat swerves and heads straight for a bridge pillar. Tae dives for the tiller and yanks it. The boat scrapes past the concrete: **ครูดดด!** Sparks.
9. **00:52** The swerve tips the boat. Mew slides down the wet floor toward the stern, where Runner #5 is clinging to the back with its legs in the water. Khao Tang is at the bow, too far away.
10. **00:55** Runner #5 grabs for Mew's ankle. Mew kicks, and it misses her by a finger's width. She grabs Lung Chan's whistle and blows: **ปรี๊ดดด!**
11. **00:56** Khao Tang comes at the whistle like a rocket. He sprints the length of the boat, leaps over Mew and clamps onto the runner's wrist: **งับ!** He shakes it like a toy. The runner loses its grip on the boat. The dog trots back proudly. (This is Khao Tang's save, and the whistle echoes Lung Chan.)
12. **00:59** The last runner, the biggest one, stands up on the canopy frame over Tae.
13. **01:00 — SLOW-MO (×0.25, about 3 s).** Tae plants a foot on the gunwale and leaps. In mid-air, with the bridge lights behind him and droplets hanging in the frame, he raises a flying elbow (ศอกกลับ). Sub: "ค่าโดยสาร… ซอมบี้ไม่รับ!" Speed snaps back to normal on impact: **โครม!!**
14. **01:04** Both of them crash through the canopy. Tae lands on his back in the boat. The runner tumbles over the side: **ตูม!** It sinks, leaving a ring of bubbles. "ตัวสุดท้าย… จมหายไปในแม่น้ำ"
15. **01:06** Silence. Tae, flat on his back, gives a thumbs-up. Mew giggles. Khao Tang licks his face.

- **Ambience:** in the canal, close wooden walls, dripping, and runners' footsteps slapping along the bank. On the river: lapping water, a distant siren far off then gone, wind. Under the bridge it gets boomy: girders creak and runners shriek above.
- **Engine:** coughs at the cold open, then a steady loud long-tail drone (it is the danger: louder = more zombies). Cut dead by the kill switch at 01:08; the breather is only water and wind. It restarts at 01:20 for the last stretch.
- **Hits:** helmet thwacks (hollow plastic), elbows and knees (dry punches), IV-pole metal clangs, canvas tearing, a lantern smashing, the boat scraping the pillar, splashes.
- **Whistle:** one sharp, long blast at 00:55: Lung Chan's whistle, the same sound as in Z05.
- **Slow-mo:** all sound drops to a low whoosh plus a heartbeat, then a huge impact as speed returns.
- **Dog:** sharp barks before the drop, a growl under the pier.
- **Bell:** none. Z08 ended on its silence, and the quiet tower at 00:03 plays over the engine coughing.
- **Bite:** total silence for 0.5 s on the black frame, then one small "กึก". A soft clock tick (the timer) runs under the last 6 s and the card.
