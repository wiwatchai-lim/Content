# กรุงเทพฯ ไม่มีพรุ่งนี้ — zombie action series (10 EP)

Pure zombie action, NO eyewear / shop / brand content at all (user, 2026-10-28).
Vertical 9:16, Thai VO + Thai subtitles, ~90 s per EP, same production pipeline as story-clips/eyesight EP01–06
(Gemini TTS → timeline → HyperFrames SVG). Each EP = one big fight set-piece + a cliffhanger into the next.
Folders: `Z01_<slug>/` … `Z10_<slug>/`, each with `script.md`.

## Tone
Fast, loud, fun — Thai action movie energy (Ong-Bak meets Train to Busan). Real Bangkok places.
Every EP: cold-open hook in 3 s → build → one fight set-piece (≥ 35 s, beat-by-beat) → breather → cliffhanger.

## Cast
- **เต้** (28) — motorbike taxi rider, ex-Muay Thai fighter. Elbows, knees, his helmet as a weapon. Jokes when scared.
- **ฝน** (26) — ER nurse. Cool head, fights with an IV pole, knows where to hit. Leader brain.
- **มิว** (10) — girl found alone in Z02. Bitten in Z04 and does not turn: she is immune → everyone wants her.
- **ข้าวตัง** — Mew's small brown dog. Barks before attacks, saves someone at least once.
- **ลุงชาญ** (61) — mall security guard, ex-soldier, Krabong (Thai staff) master. Joins Z05, dies a hero in Z08.
- **เสี่ยเปา** — gang boss holding a mall (Z06). Wants Mew to sell to "the people on the ship". Bitten in Z06, returns as the brute boss in Z10.

## Zombie rules
- Walkers: slow by day, hunt by sound. Runners: at night they sprint. Brutes (ตัวยักษ์): rare, huge, armoured by junk.
- Headshot / heavy hit to the head drops them. Bite = turns in 1–3 min (except Mew).
- Fire and loud noise pull hordes.

## Arc
Day 1 outbreak at Victory Monument → hide → hospital → learn Mew is immune → radio: navy ship at Phra Chulachomklao Fort
(Samut Prakan) leaves at dawn of Day 3 → cross the city → river → ship.
