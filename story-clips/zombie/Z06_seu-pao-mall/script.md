# Z06 — ห้างของเสี่ย

Series: กรุงเทพฯ ไม่มีพรุ่งนี้ (see ../SERIES.md). Thai VO + Thai subtitles, notes in English.
Picks up Z05's cliffhanger: the radio said the navy ship at ป้อมพระจุลจอมเกล้า leaves at dawn on Day 3, and Seu Pao's pickup was cruising past the car-park ramp.

- **Logline:** Seu Pao's gang offers the group a ride to the fort and a safe night in his fortified mall, but the "safe night" is a trap to sell Mew. When the gang's generator and karaoke pull a night horde through the glass, it becomes a three-way fight between survivors, gangsters and runners on a dead escalator.
- **Length:** ~90 s (cold open 0–3, build 3–22, fight 22–64, breather 64–76, cliffhanger 76–90)
- **Clock:** Day 2, 19:20 → 19:31 (night: runners sprint). Countdown chip under the clock `เหลือ 34:xx:xx`.
- **Location:** Seu Pao's mall, an old wholesale clothes mall near ประตูน้ำ. Rolling shutters welded shut, sandbags, a roaring generator by the main glass doors, fairy lights, a karaoke screen in the 1st-floor food court, a stopped escalator in a central atrium, mannequins everywhere.
- **On screen at once (max):** Tae, Fon, Mew, Khao Tang, Lung Chan, Seu Pao + 2 gang members + 3 runners (in fight shots, keep to 5–6 figures per frame).

## Fight choreography (0:32–1:04, 32 s + breach build 0:26–0:32 = ≥ 35 s set-piece)
Geography (keep it readable): ground-floor atrium → stopped escalator (centre) → 1st-floor food court on top. Cage is ground floor, left. Glass doors right.
1. **0:32** Glass bursts; three runners sprint in on all fours, leap onto the generator; one gang member swings a bat — `เคร้ง!` — misses, hits the generator, sparks.
2. **0:35** Khao Tang squeezes past a distracted guard, snaps the key ring off his belt loop, sprints back and drops it through the cage bars. Fon unlocks. `แก๊ก!`
3. **0:38** Cage swings open into a gang member's face. `โครม!` Tae, line 12 joke, while stepping over him.
4. **0:40** Gang member #2 charges Tae with a machete. Tae catches the wrist, Muay Thai clinch, knee to the ribs `ตุ้บ!`, elbow to the jaw `ปั้ก!`. Machete spins away and sticks in a mannequin's head. `ฉึก!`
5. **0:42** Runner leaps at Tae's back. Fon swings the IV pole like a bat — hook end clips the runner's head mid-air. `เคร้ง!` It drops at Tae's feet. Tae: "ขอบคุณครับพยาบาล" (white mini sub).
6. **0:44** The gang realise the runners don't care who's who: a runner tackles a gang member; the others switch from fighting the group to fighting for their lives. Three-way chaos shot (top-down): orange vest, teal scrubs, black tank tops, pale runners.
7. **0:47** Lung Chan plants himself at the foot of the stopped escalator. Runners pour at him two at a time. Krabong spin: strike left head `ป๊อก!`, strike right head `ป๊อก!`, butt-end jab to a third `ปั้ก!`. They tumble down the steps like dominoes.
8. **0:50** Up on the food-court balcony: Seu Pao drags Mew by the backpack strap with one hand, golf club in the other.
9. **0:52** Tae runs up the escalator side-rail (not the steps — Lung Chan is holding those), sliding back a step every two, hands slipping. Near-miss: a gang bat smashes the rubber handrail an inch from his fingers. `ตุ้บ!`
10. **0:55** Mew screams line 16, bites Seu Pao's wrist (kid bite, not a zombie bite — he yelps and lets go of the strap but grabs her collar).
11. **0:56** Seu Pao swings the golf club at Tae as he crests the top. Tae ducks, the club takes off his helmet visor. `เคร้ง!` Tae head-butts Seu Pao's belly with the helmet. `ตุ้บ!` Seu Pao staggers back to the balcony rail.
12. **0:58 SLOW-MO** (×0.25, colours drained except Mew's yellow shirt and Fon's teal): Mew is pulled backward over the rail with Seu Pao; Fon, from the escalator, swings the IV pole up — the hook catches Mew's backpack strap — Fon heaves. Mew flies back onto the balcony floor, Tae catches her. Seu Pao is left hanging on the rail alone, gold chain floating up in slow motion. Line 17 over it.
13. **1:01** Speed snaps back. Seu Pao drags himself over the rail — and a runner climbing the outside of the balcony clamps onto his hand. `งั่บ!` Line 18. Seu Pao smashes it off with the golf club `โครม!` — it falls to the atrium floor.
14. **1:02** Lung Chan below: "ขึ้นไปห้องเก็บของ! ไป!" He backs up the escalator step by step, staff spinning, Khao Tang at his heels.
15. **1:03** Everyone piles into the storeroom; Tae shoulder-slams the door shut, a runner's arm reaching through the gap — Fon slams the door twice on it, it retracts. `ปัง! ปัง!`
