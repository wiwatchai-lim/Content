# Z10 — รุ่งเช้าที่ปากน้ำ (season finale)

- **Series:** กรุงเทพฯ ไม่มีพรุ่งนี้ (see ../SERIES.md). Pure zombie action, no eyewear / shop / brand content.
- **Logline:** Tae has under three minutes before the bite takes him. The navy ship is lowering its gangway, but the soldiers won't let a bitten man aboard. Then a cargo container bursts open on the pier: เสี่ยเปา kept his date "ที่ท่าเรือ", now a three-metre brute in junk armour. Tae spends his last human minutes on one final fight so Mew can reach the ship.
- **Length:** ~112 s (01:56 including the tease card), 9:16, Thai VO + Thai subtitles.
- **Clock:** Day 3, 05:54 → 06:14 (sunrise at about 06:12). On-screen clock chip top-left. Tae's **bite timer** chip (red) carries over from Z09: it reads 02:41 at the open and runs down through the episode (time is compressed by cuts; the chip jumps at each cut) to 00:00 at the end.
- **Location:** the pier at ป้อมพระจุลจอมเกล้า, Samut Prakan, at the mouth of the Chao Phraya: a long concrete pier with bollards, a small dock crane with a hook on a chain, a hanging concrete counterweight, two oil drums, a blue shipping container, one old fort cannon at the land end. The grey navy ship sits at the far end with its gangway half down.
- **Follows Z09:** Tae was bitten on the forearm under this pier while saving Mew. He said "แค่… ข่วนน่า". The timer started at 03:00.
- **Pays off Z06:** Seu Pao, bitten on the hand, laughed and said "ไว้เจอกันที่ท่าเรือนะ เด็กน้อย" before backing into the dark.
- **Cast on screen:** เต้ (helmet, red riding vest, the bitten forearm wrapped in Fon's tourniquet; grey veins creep up his neck as the timer runs down), ฝน (scrubs, IV pole), มิว (yellow raincoat, bandage on her **left** forearm, Lung Chan's whistle), ข้าวตัง, **เสี่ยเปา brute** (see below), and 2 soldiers on the gangway (simple helmet + rifle silhouettes, no faces). At most 4 figures in any shot besides the dog.
- **Seu Pao brute design:** the same silhouette cues as Z06, swollen to three metres: the torn loud floral shirt, the thick gold chain sunk into his neck, the slicked hair now patchy, the toothpick still in his teeth, his bent golf driver fused into his right fist like a club. Junk armour: a car door strapped across his chest, an oil-drum lid on one shoulder, chains. The Z06 bite on his hand is now black and huge. **Weak spot:** the backs of his knees, where there is no armour.

## Fight choreography (00:36–01:24, 48 s)

1. **00:36** Tae opens with Muay Thai: a teep to the stomach, then a right knee, a left elbow. **ปั้ก! ตุ้บ! ปั้ก!** Every hit lands on the car door. **เคร้ง! เคร้ง!** Seu Pao doesn't move. He spits his toothpick at Tae. Tae: "เข่า! ศอก! …ไม่สะเทือนเลยว่ะ"
2. **00:40** Seu Pao swings the fused golf driver like a tee shot. Tae ducks, and it whistles over his helmet (near-miss), so close it scrapes a line of sparks off the helmet top.
3. **00:42** The backswing catches Tae: **โครม!** He flies across the pier into the two oil drums, and they roll away clanging. **เคร้ง ๆ ๆ**
4. **00:45** Seu Pao raises the club to finish him. Fon runs in from the side and jams the IV pole into the club's swing arc, a blocking bar. **เคร้ง!!** The pole bends but holds.
5. **00:47** Fon, under his weight, shouts: "ข้อพับเข่า! ตรงนั้นไม่มีเกราะ!" She sees it: car-door armour on the front, nothing behind the knees.
6. **00:49** Tae kicks one of the rolling drums straight into Seu Pao's shins. The brute stumbles half a step. **ตึง!**
7. **00:52** Fon slides between his legs (near-miss: his fist slams the concrete where her head just was, **ตูม!**, concrete chips fly) and swings the pole into the back of his right knee. **ปั้ก!** He drops onto one knee. The pier shakes.
8. **00:56** Tae pushes himself up. His hand shakes; the grey veins are at his collar. Timer "01:10". He shakes it off: "ยังก่อน… ขอต่อยให้จบยกนี้ก่อน"
9. **01:00** Tae runs up the drum, onto the brute's bent knee, and drives an elbow down onto the top of the skull. **ปั้ก!!** Seu Pao staggers, but his free hand shoots out past Tae...
10. **01:04** ...and scoops Mew off the pier. He lifts her toward his open jaws, toothpick-gap grin. Mew's whistle dangles.
11. **01:07** Mew blows Lung Chan's whistle in his face: **ปรี๊ดดด!** Seu Pao flinches from the noise. Khao Tang leaps off a bollard and bites the black bitten hand from Z06: **งับ!** The hand opens and Mew drops. Fon catches her. (Khao Tang's save.)
12. **01:09** Tae grabs the dock crane's dangling hook and chain, swings around behind the brute, and loops the chain twice around Seu Pao's armoured chest and the car door. **กริ๊ง กริ๊ง กร๊าง!** He hooks it back onto itself.
13. **01:11** Tae, to Fon: points at the crane's red brake lever. Then to Seu Pao: "เสี่ย… ขึ้นเรือผิดลำแล้ว!" Fon slams the lever. **แกร๊ก!**
14. **01:13** Above, the concrete counterweight starts to drop toward the water at the pier's edge. The chain snaps tight and drags Seu Pao backward, his heels gouging the concrete. He digs in and roars. He's holding.
15. **01:15 — SLOW-MO (×0.25, about 4 s).** The sun breaks over the sea behind Seu Pao. Tae runs three steps and leaps: a flying knee (เข่าลอย), silhouetted against the sun. The grey veins are on his face now. Sub: "พระอาทิตย์โผล่… เข่าลอยครั้งสุดท้าย". The knee meets the brute's chin.
16. **01:19** Speed snaps back: **โครม!!!** Seu Pao's grip breaks. The chain whips him off the pier edge, he and the counterweight hit the sea together, **ตูม!!**, and a huge white splash rises.
17. **01:21** The gold chain, torn off in the fall, lands on the pier and slides to a stop at Mew's feet. **ชิ้ง.** A ring of bubbles in the water, then nothing.
18. **01:23** Tae lands, rolls, and ends up sitting against a bollard. He gives a weak thumbs-up. Timer "00:14".

## Sound notes (no music; the user adds music later)

- **Ambience:** sea wind, waves slapping the pier piles, gulls arriving once the sun is up, the ship's engines humming low, a deep ship horn at 00:04 and again faintly before the card.
- **Heartbeat:** under the whole episode, tied to the timer. It starts strong and slows and gets heavier as the timer drops; it is louder in the quiet moments.
- **Seu Pao:** heavy stomps that shake the pier (low thud plus a camera shake), a wet roar, the clank of his junk armour as he moves, metal chains clinking.
- **Hits:** elbows and knees on the car door = metallic **เคร้ง**; on flesh = dry thumps. Golf club whoosh. Oil drums rolling and clanging. IV pole bending (metal groan). Concrete cracking under his fist. Crane brake lever clack, chain rattling taut, a huge splash.
- **Whistle:** one long blast at 01:07, Lung Chan's whistle, the same sound as in Z05 and Z09.
- **Slow-mo:** everything drops to wind plus a slow heartbeat, then the impact as speed returns.
- **Ending:** after the last splash, only waves. At timer 00:00, cut **all** sound for about 3 s, heartbeat included. On the eyes opening there is one single heartbeat, then black. The radio line is dry, with crackle and a little static.

## Cliffhanger + Season 2 tease card

- **Cliffhanger:** the timer hits 00:00 with Mew's blood on Tae's bite. Tae's head drops. Silence. Then his eyes open, and we cut to black before we can see whether he is still Tae. Over the fade-up of the card, the ship's radio, cold and flat: "ยืนยัน… พบตัวเด็กแล้ว". The people on the ship are the buyers Seu Pao meant to sell Mew to in Z06.
