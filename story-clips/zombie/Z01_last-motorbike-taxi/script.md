# Z01 — วินคันสุดท้าย

- **Series:** กรุงเทพฯ ไม่มีพรุ่งนี้ (see ../SERIES.md). Pure zombie action, no eyewear / shop / brand content.
- **Logline:** At rush hour the dead rise at Victory Monument; motorbike-taxi rider เต้ rides into the chaos, fights up onto a jammed bus roof with his helmet and knees to save ER nurse ฝน — then the radio says the scratched must be avoided, and ฝน's arm is bleeding.
- **Length:** ~90 s (VO paced for the ×1.08 timeline speed-up).
- **Clock:** Day 1, 17:40 → 17:52 (clock chip top-left; sunset orange → dusk purple).
- **Location:** อนุสาวรีย์ชัยฯ roundabout, a jammed red city bus, then a shuttered shophouse on ถนนราชวิถี.
- **Voice:** one narrator voice (Gemini TTS, Sulafat) reads everything; character lines are in quotes with the name in the subtitle (`เต้:` / `ฝน:`).
- **On screen at once:** max 1 hero + 3 zombies in close shots; the horde is drawn as a dark silhouette mass with glowing-white eye dots.

## Fight choreography (bus roof, 27.0–66.0 = 39 s)
1. **Ramp (27.0):** เต้ guns the bike up a pickup's dropped tailgate; the bike lands on the bus roof sideways and skids to a stop right beside ฝน. `โครม!`
2. **Landing kick (28.5):** the skid knocks zombie A's fingers off the roof edge; it falls back onto a car bonnet.
3. **Helmet swing (30.0):** zombie B gets its torso over the edge; เต้ grabs the helmet by the strap and swings it round like a flail into B's head. `ตุ้บ!` B drops.
4. **Flying knee (33.0):** zombie C climbs up through the roof hatch; เต้ runs two steps and hits a Muay Thai flying knee to its chin; C falls back down the hatch onto another zombie inside. `พลั่ก!`
5. **Funny exchange (36.0–42.0):** ฝน yells "hit the head"; เต้, already rubbing his knee, answers he has been hitting heads for ten years. ฝน slams the hatch shut with her foot without looking.
6. **Ankle grab (42.0):** zombie D reaches out of a broken side window and grabs ฝน's ankle; she slips, IV pole clatters, and her left forearm drags across the jagged window edge right next to D's clawing hand (shown as a 4-frame blur, so the viewer can't tell glass from nails — Z02 reveals it was glass); near-miss as D's teeth snap an inch from her shoe.
7. **IV-pole strike (45.0):** ฝน spins the pole and drives the wheeled base straight down into D's face. `เคร้ง!` D lets go.
8. **Slow-mo elbow (48.0–56.0):** zombie E leaps from a truck roof onto เต้'s back. Slow-mo: E airborne, mouth open; เต้ sees it in the reflection of ฝน's eyes, pivots, spinning back elbow to the temple. Back to full speed: `โป๊ก!` E flies off the roof.
9. **Noise problem (56.0):** a car alarm goes off under the falling zombie; the map shows the horde turning toward the noise.
10. **Exit (59.0–66.0):** ฝน jumps on the back of the bike; เต้ rides off the front of the bus onto a taxi roof, then down to the road; zombie hands claw at ฝน's arms as they pass. Bike weaves out between cars.
