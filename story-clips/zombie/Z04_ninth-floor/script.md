## 3. Fight choreography (0:27–1:09, 42 s)

Shaft = tall dark rectangle with two vertical cable lines, a steel ladder on one wall and door slits every floor. Stairwell = zig-zag cross-section.

1. **0:27** The falling zombie drops past them; Fon pulls Mew flat against the ladder; it clips Tae's shoulder and drops down the shaft. **"วู้ม… โครม!"** (far below)
2. **0:29** They climb: Fon first with Mew on her back (Khao Tang zipped in Mew's hoodie, head poking out), Tae last with the IV pole through his belt.
3. **0:30** A floor-4 door slit bursts open; arms reach for Fon. Fon: "จับสายไว้! อย่าปล่อย!" She swings off the ladder onto the cable; the hands miss by a hair. **"ฟิ้ว!"**
4. **0:32** Tae, below, drives the IV pole through the gap into the zombie's head and shoves it back. **"เคร้ง!"**
5. **0:34** A zombie climbs down the ladder from above toward Fon. Fon kicks the rung under its foot; it slips and hangs by one hand. **"กึก!"**
6. **0:36** Tae, hand over hand on the cable: "ปีนเชือกครั้งสุดท้าย สอบพละ ป.6!"
7. **0:38** The hanging zombie drops onto Tae. He lets go with one hand and swings sideways; it falls past, teeth scraping his helmet. Near miss. **"กร้วบ!"**
8. **0:41** The stuck cabin above creaks, cables shake. Fon forces the floor-6 doors open with her shoulder. **"แกร๊ก!"** VO: "ชั้นหก… ออกทางบันได"
9. **0:44** Stairwell, floor 6→7: two walkers on the landing. Tae charges up, Muay Thai knee into the first one's chin. **"ตุ้บ!"**
10. **0:46** The second grabs his helmet strap from behind and pulls him backward down the stairs. Fon catches his collar one-handed.
11. **0:48.4** Fon: "ซ้าย! หัวมัน!" Tae drives a left elbow back into the zombie's head. **"ป้าก!"** It tumbles down the flight. **"โครม โครม โครม!"**
12. **0:51–0:54 SLOW-MO:** landing 7→8: a runner in hospital scrubs leaps down a whole flight at Fon. Slow-mo: Fon pivots and plants the IV pole's base on the step; the runner's own momentum carries its head into the pole's hook. Mew's hair floats, Khao Tang's ears flap. Speed snaps back. **"เคร้ง!!"**
13. **0:55** Tae, looking at the scrubs: "ใครอยู่เวรดึก ยกมือหน่อย!" Fon glares at him.
14. **0:57** Floor 8→9: walkers pour in from the floor-8 door below. The heroes run up; Tae rips a fire extinguisher off the wall.
15. **0:59** Tae sprays down the stairs: white cloud, zombies stumble blind; he throws the empty can, it bounces off a head. **"ฟู่ววว! ป๊อก!"**
16. **1:00.4** VO: "ชั้นเก้า! ประตูอยู่ตรงนั้น!" Fon gets Mew through the floor-9 fire door.
17. **1:02** A zombie bursts out of the cloud and grabs Tae's ankle; he falls on the top step. **"ตุ้บ!"**
18. **1:04** Mew: "พี่เต้ ข้างหลัง!" She runs back and pulls Tae's arm. The zombie lunges for Tae's hand; Mew's left forearm is in the way. Hard cut on the lunge, a white flash, no gore. **"งับ!"**
19. **1:06** Khao Tang bites the zombie's ear and hangs on; Tae turns and helmet-smashes it down the stairs. **"ปั้ก!"**
20. **1:08** Fon drags them both in and slams the door (into the 1:09 shot).
