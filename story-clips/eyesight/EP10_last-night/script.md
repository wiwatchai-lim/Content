# EP10 — คืนสุดท้าย (season 1 finale, follows EP09's escape into the fog)

Thai only. Same look and pipeline as EP01–06 (see ../SERIES.md). ~110 s, including a 7 s pause in the voice for the viewer's near-point test.
Science: the lens inside the eye stiffens with age, so from around 40 focusing up close gets harder and people hold the phone or paper
farther away ("arms too short") — presbyopia (สายตายาวตามวัย), normal ageing, not a disease; reading glasses or progressive lenses fix it
(measure first). Get an eye exam about once a year: sight changes slowly without you noticing, and with both eyes open a good eye can
hide a weak one (cover one eye at a time); if near reading gets blurry, see an ophthalmologist or optometrist.
Lens card: young lens bulges/flattens freely, the 40+ lens barely changes; a near dot focuses on the retina (young) vs behind it (40+) = blur.
No distances are given anywhere (the test and the age markers are qualitative).

Test screen: "ลองเองตอนนี้" — 1 hold your index finger at arm's length, 2 bring it slowly toward your face watching the fingerprint,
3 stop where it starts to blur. A finger slides toward a face in profile, blurs at a red dashed "จุดใกล้สุดที่ชัด" line; a gold bar fills
during the 7 s pause; note "ทำช้าๆ ไม่ทัน กดหยุดคลิปไว้ก่อนได้", then "ลองคร่าวๆ เท่านั้น ไม่ใช่การตรวจวัดสายตา". After the pause the
markers อายุน้อย / ราว 40 / อายุมากขึ้น appear farther and farther from the face.

Story: 05:20 you reach the last house at the edge of the village; an old uncle pulls you in and bolts the door; it is already in the yard.
