# EP04 — ถ้าเช้าแล้ว มันยังไม่ไป (follows EP03's end card)

Thai only. Same look and pipeline as EP01–03 (see ../README.md). 87 s.
Science: after a night in the dark the pupils are wide and the eyes dark-adapted, so stepping into morning light whites everything out →
stop at the door, squint, shade your eyes; vision mostly recovers in about a minute. Never stare at the sun, even at sunrise: the eye's lens
focuses it to a spot that can burn the retina (solar retinopathy), and the retina has no pain nerves, so you don't feel it.
UV400 sunglasses protect the eyes in daylight but do not make looking at the sun safe.

Lines: audio/tts_all.py (20 lines). The take read pauses fairly evenly (inside a line ~0.8 s, between lines ~1.2–1.5 s, plus three odd
~6 s silences that the split drops); audio/split.py groups phrases by count (PHRASES). Line 5 came out as three phrases.
Twist: in the low morning sun the tree, the pole, the fence and you all cast long shadows the same way — the figure at the gate casts none.
End card teases EP5 "ถ้ามันอยู่ในกระจก".
