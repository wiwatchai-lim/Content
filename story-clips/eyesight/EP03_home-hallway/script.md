# EP03 — ทางเดินในบ้าน (follows EP02's end card "ถ้ามันตามคุณไปถึงบ้าน")

Thai only. Same look and pipeline as EP01/02 (see ../README.md). 97 s, including a 4.6 s pause for the viewer's blind-spot test.
Science: dark spots that move exactly with your gaze are afterimages (they fade; blink slowly); a sudden shower of floaters or flashes
in the corner of the eye can mean a retinal tear → see an eye doctor now; each eye has a blind spot where the optic nerve leaves
(no photoreceptors), and the other eye fills it in when both are open.
Test screen: cross at x 160, red dot at x 940 (≈4.7 cm apart on a 6.5 cm-wide phone screen) → the dot vanishes at about 15–20 cm
with the left eye closed (blind spot ≈15° to the side).

Lines: audio/tts_all.py (19 lines). The take read every pause alike and ran two phrases of line 9 together, so audio/split.py
uses the phrase counts heard in the take (PHRASES, matched by phrase length).
Twist: with both eyes open, the figure was standing at the right side of the hallway all along — about 15° right of where you looked, i.e. in the open right eye's blind spot.
End card teases EP4 "ถ้าเช้าแล้ว มันยังไม่ไป".
