# EP05 — ถ้ามันอยู่ในกระจก (follows EP04's end card)

Thai only. Same look and pipeline as EP01–03 (see ../README.md). 96 s, including a 7 s pause in the voice for the viewer's Troxler test.
Science: without glasses a pinhole (fingers curled to a tiny hole, or squinting) blocks the off-axis rays, so the blur circle on the
retina shrinks and the image sharpens — works for short and long sight, an emergency trick, not a fix. Staring at one point in dim
light without moving the eyes makes what is around it fade (Troxler fading) and your own face warp in a mirror: the brain stops
reporting what doesn't change; move the eyes and blink and it comes back.
Test screen: centre dot + four very soft pastel blobs, all static (only the thin progress bar moves); stare ~14 s → the blobs fade.

Lines: audio/tts_all.py (18 lines). This take kept ~3.5 s between lines; audio/split.py still groups phrases per line (PHRASES).
Blurred shape behind your reflection = a towel on the back of the door (the blurred copy hides its stripes and darkens it).
Twist: you blink; through the slit of your eyelids the reflection's eyes stay wide open.
