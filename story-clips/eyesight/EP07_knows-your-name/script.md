# EP07 — ถ้ามันรู้ชื่อคุณ (follows EP06's end card)

Thai only. Same look and pipeline as EP01–06 (see ../SERIES.md). 103.5 s, including an 8 s pause in the voice for the viewer's dominant-eye test.
Science: in the dark the colour cells (cones) almost stop working and the eye runs on rods — sensitive to light but colour-blind. Rods barely
respond to red, so red things look dark, almost black, while blue-green things look relatively brighter (Purkinje shift) → don't trust
colours in the dark. Peripheral vision catches movement fast but sees little detail or colour: it tells you something moved, not what it is.
Dominant eye: for sighting through a small hole people tend to use their dominant eye without noticing.
Test screen: make a triangle with both hands at arm's length, both eyes open, frame a distant object; close the left eye (3-2-1), then the
right (3-2-1); the eye that keeps the object inside the triangle is the dominant eye. The picture shows a right-eye-dominant example
(the clock jumps out when the right eye closes) and says so.
