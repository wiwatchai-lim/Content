# EP01 — หลงป่าตอนตีสอง (draft v1)

Language: Thai (exception to the English-only house rule, user 2026-10-26).
Look: flat 2D vector, drawn in code (HyperFrames + SVG), night forest palette.
Science: dark adaptation (~20–30 min, reset by a bright flash), rods are almost blind to deep red light,
the fovea (centre of the eye) has almost no rods → look slightly to the side in the dark (averted vision).
