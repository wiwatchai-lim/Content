# 15-second beat clip — titanium half-rim frame (Time Optic)

**Job type:** Product intro / demo (CONTENT_TYPES #6), made with `beat-clip`. Format 1080×1920, English only, no price
(none was given; I ask once "มีราคา/โปรไหมคะ" and leave it out if the answer is no). The upload file has SFX only and the user adds the song in TikTok.

**Inputs locked before building**
- Frame: a real stock row (`data/frames/frames-stock-*.csv`) with `_check` status `ยืนยัน`. The words "titanium" and "half-rim / semi-rimless" appear on screen **only if** the maker's page in `_check` says so, and never "hypoallergenic" or "lightest" (C26: many titanium frames use mixed materials). If several stock colours exist, all of them go in the clip.
- Photos: front `_1`, three-quarter `_2`, side `_3`. For punch-ins of the hinge, bridge and brow-bar I need sources ≥ 1500 px. A missing angle is filled with a verified web photo of the same model and colour, and nothing is upscaled past its real pixels.
- Lens + coating from the Pairing rules. A half-rim (nylor) frame needs a groovable material such as Polycarbonate or Trivex. Names and one maker line are copied from `data/lenses|coatings/<brand>.md`.
- Song: **Silent Desire** (90.8 BPM, moody minimal R&B / luxury, used once on 2026-09-25) fits a fine titanium frame. Backup: **Late Night Loops** (80.7 BPM, unused). Beat B = 60/90.8 = 0.661 s, 22 beats = 14.54 s plus a 0.46 s hold = 15.0 s. `song_start` is set so the drop lands on beat 4.
- Base file: a copy of an approved kit's `hf/index.html` (e.g. `2026-09-25_ph-5246d`, also on Silent Desire). It keeps `B`, `b(n)`, `bounceIn/hop/bounceOut`, `whip` and the letter-slice wordmark, and the moves are re-planned so they differ from that clip.

Layout: every piece of type and every logo stays inside x 60–940 / y 220–1480, with key text in y 300–1400. The frame brand logo sits at left 64 / top 64 for the whole clip, with explicit width + height. The glasses sit large in the middle band, and the detail that matters in each punch-in stays inside the zone. There is no store line art. Backgrounds stay calm (no neon), and the moves mix bounce, mask, wipe, warp and glint, so the clip is not a repeat of the last one.

## Sound plan
- `clip-audio.json`: `{seconds: 15.0, song: "Silent Desire.m4a", song_start: <drop on beat 4>, fade_out: 0.4, sfx: [...]}` with the cues listed above. SFX come from the HyperFrames sfx folder. At most one hit per beat, and the whooshes sit a few ms before the cut.
- `python tools/beat-audio.py <kit> export/_silent.mp4` produces `clip-hf.mp4` (SFX only, for upload) and `clip-hf_preview-song.mp4` (song + SFX). The preview is mastered to about -14 LUFS with true peak ≤ -1.5 dBTP (MIX_PLAYBOOK). Impacts are controlled with the limiter, not by clipping.
- The caption gives the TikTok sound name and its start time, so the user can match the song in the app.

## Checks before delivery
1. `memory.py brief` was obeyed (house clip rules, no floating temple, price only from the user).
2. Data: frame row verified, model, colour, lens and coating names match `data/` exactly, no invented claims (C26), no price.
3. Cutouts: alpha median ≥ ~230 (rebuild if semi-transparent). Titanium and silver parts on white often lose opacity, so those layers go on light grounds only. Lens layer present. `export/preview.png` checked by eye.
4. `npx --yes hyperframes@0.8.59 check` gives 0 errors, then render.
5. Contact sheet, one frame per beat, with the safe zone drawn (`drawbox=x=60:y=220:w=880:h=1260`), plus stills at the hook (t=0) and the drop (2.64 s). Type readable and inside the box, nothing overlapping, glasses sharp and not upscaled, logo in the corner, wordmark gap right, "Time Optic" spelled as two words.
6. Retention review (RETENTION_REVIEW B1–B6): frame 0 has one strong focal point, product and brand cue in the first 1–2 s, a change on every beat with no dead stretch, one clear peak (the drop), an end that closes on the wordmark.
7. Audio: loudness and true peak measured with `ffmpeg -af ebur128`/loudnorm on the preview. I can't hear it, so I say so to the user.
8. Independent `creative-critic` sub-agent, then fix critical and major findings before showing.
9. Hand over `kit.md`, `caption.md` (English, data-only, hashtags, sound + start), the `log/kits.csv` row, and the "used for" line in `music/songs.md`. Open the preview and report in Thai. After the user reacts, run `memory.py add` with their words.
