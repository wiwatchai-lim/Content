# C5 — Critique: Instagram caption

| # | Location | Problem | Why (rule / evidence, source) | Fix | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Line 2 | "เลนส์ของเรารักษาสายตาสั้นให้หายได้ภายใน 30 วัน" | False medical claim: no lens cures myopia; even myopia-control lenses only *slow* progression in children (claims C19 "partly"; FACT_CHECK: "รักษา/หายขาด" banned). Promising a result within a time frame = Meta health clickbait (ETHICS §6). Thai CPA s.22(1)(2); s.47 penalties. | Delete. If relevant: "เลนส์ช่วยให้มองชัดตามค่าสายตา" or myopia-control wording from C19 with source. | critical |
| 2 | Line 2 | "รับรองโดยจักษุแพทย์ระดับโลก" | Unnamed, unprovable endorsement = deceptive testimonial / authority (FTC 2022 dark pattern; ETHICS §5); claimed certification you can't prove is deemed false (CPA s.28). | Delete, or name a real, consenting professional with evidence on file. | critical |
| 3 | Line 1 | "เหลือแค่ 2 อันสุดท้ายในประเทศไทย" | False scarcity (ETHICS §1.2 "ห้าม … เหลือ 2 ชิ้นสุดท้าย ทั้งที่ไม่จริง"); "ในประเทศไทย" is unverifiable. | State real stock only, or nothing. | critical |
| 4 | Line 1 | "ถ้าไม่ซื้อวันนี้คุณจะเสียใจไปตลอดชีวิต 😱😱" + "⚠️ด่วนที่สุด!!!" | Pressure/fear with no information or action step; clickbait does not raise curiosity vs a normal headline (Molina 2021, ETHICS §2); fear without efficacy is weak (ANTI_PATTERNS). Shouting register harms trust for an optical shop. | Calm, concrete first line: what the product is and why it matters. | major |
| 5 | Line 3 | "ดวงตาคือหน้าต่างของหัวใจ" — วิลเลียม เชกสเปียร์ | Misattributed and distorted: the "eyes are the window of the soul" idea is an old proverb (often traced to Cicero / the Bible's "the eye is the lamp of the body"), not a Shakespeare line; "หัวใจ" also changes it. Rule: no misattributed quotes; unsourced quote → paraphrase without a name (philosophy §7; ANTI_PATTERNS). | Drop the quote or use it unattributed as the shop's own line. | major |
| 6 | Hashtags | 31 hashtags | Instagram allows max 30 per post (platform limit; over it the caption/tag set can fail). Most are irrelevant spam (#nature #selfie #cute #summer #followme #like4like) — they mislead the topic signal and look spammy; #tiktok/#fyp belong to another platform. | 5–10 relevant tags: brand/model, lens type, #แว่นหัวหิน / location, #timeoptic. | major |
| 7 | Whole caption | No product, no lens name, no shop/location, no real CTA | A caption must tell what is shown (frame + lens from `data/`) and the next step (chat, visit, branch). | Add product line + one CTA ("ทักแชตนัดวัดสายตา"). | major |
| 8 | Language | Thai caption | House rule: posts and their captions are English-only, except the named Thai series (CLAUDE.md; beat-clip captions English). | Confirm with the user; default English (or EN + TH only if asked). | minor |
| 9 | Emoji | ⚠️ ×2, 😱 ×2 | Warning/fear emoji reinforce the scare tactic; ⚠️ reads as a safety alert. | 0–2 neutral emoji. | minor |

**What is good:** the Thai eyewear hashtags (#แว่น #แว่นตา #แว่นสายตา #ร้านแว่น) are relevant to local search and can stay; the caption is short enough to read in a feed.

Safe rewrite direction (example): "[Frame brand + model from data] · paired with [lens + coating from data]. Visit Time Optic Hua Hin or chat to book an eye measurement. #timeoptic #oakley #แว่นหัวหิน #แว่นสายตา #ร้านแว่น"
