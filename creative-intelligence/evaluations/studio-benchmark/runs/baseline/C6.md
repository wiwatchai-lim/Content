# C6 — Critique: storyboard "ร้านแว่นตีสอง" EP02 scene 3

| # | Location | Problem | Why (rule / evidence, source) | Fix | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shot 2 | 6 frames (0.25 s) for a 14-word Thai line | The line is ~40 base characters → ≈3.5–4 s at the ~11 Thai chars/s fallback (SCRIPT_CHECKER); a subtitle cue must last ≥0.8 s and ≤17 cps (cognitive-science §4.2 item 1, Netflix Thai). 6 F can't hold the line, the lip-sync or the subtitle. Probably "6 s" was meant. | Time the shot from the real TTS take (timeline.py): ≈ 90–110 F incl. a 6–12 F face change *before* the line and a hold after (GUIDE acting). | critical |
| 2 | Shot 3 | Camera crosses the line; ช่างแว่น jumps to the left **and faces left** | 180° rule: in a dialogue A stays left, B right in every shot unless the move is shown (GUIDE "Screen direction & continuity"). Facing left also breaks eyeline match — he now looks away from ยายจันทร์. Viewers lose who is talking to whom. | Keep the camera on the shot-1 side of the line: OTS over ยายจันทร์'s shoulder (she is frame-left, back to camera) with ช่างแว่น frame-right, facing left toward her. If a cross is wanted, show it (camera move through the line or a neutral head-on shot first). | critical |
| 3 | Shot 4 | ยายจันทร์ without glasses | Continuity: she wears the gold round glasses through EP01–EP02 — an identity marker of the character and the hero prop of the episode (CONTINUITY_SYSTEM `identity_markers`, `props[hero]`). Removing them with no on-screen action is an error — and her line is literally about not wanting to change them. | Glasses on; if taking them off is a story beat, show the action (she lifts them off, holds them) in its own shot. | major |
| 4 | Shot 4 | Hair tied up (loose in shot 1) | Same scene, seconds later — continuity break (CONTINUITY_SYSTEM state_changes; GUIDE "solid drawing: same volumes … per character in every shot"). | Same hair state as shot 1 (use the same puppet/state). | major |
| 5 | Shot 4 grade | Cold daylight blue in a warm night scene "with no story reason" | Colour script: palette changes *are* the story arc, decided per scene (GUIDE §6); a random grade jump reads as a time/location change. (A cool accent can be motivated — "a ghost is the coolest thing in a warm room" — but then it must be designed as a beat, not a whole-shot daylight grade.) | Keep warm night tungsten for the scene; if her ghost nature is the reveal, add a subtle cool rim/haze on her only and note the reason in the colour script. | major |
| 6 | Sound — music | Music bed at full level under dialogue | Voice must sit ≥10 LU above music while speaking; leave space under the voice, the mix ducks the bed (MIX_PLAYBOOK §3; GUIDE sound). Also: in this series the user adds music later. | No music bed in our export (or ducked ≥10–15 LU if a temp bed is used for review). | major |
| 7 | Sound — room tone | No room tone | "Room tone in every interior; ambience changes with every location" (GUIDE sound). Silence under dialogue sounds like a dead edit. | Add shop interior tone (low hum, clock, canal outside) at −36 to −32 LUFS. | minor |
| 8 | Sound — footsteps | Not synced | Sound may not lead picture by more than ~45 ms (ITU; cognitive-science §4.2 item 4); unsynced footsteps break the illusion. | Place footstep SFX on contact frames (or up to ~125 ms late). | minor |
| 9 | Shot 1 | 3 s static wide is fine, but no motion note | Nothing static > ~6 s; add life (push, blink, dust) (GUIDE editing). Here it's short enough — just add idle motion. | Slow push-in or idle blinks. | minor |
| 10 | Characters | "ช่างแว่น (optician)" — unnamed role | The series' shopkeeper is ต้น (Ton), with a voice/character bible (SERIES.md; CHARACTER_VOICE_BIBLE `ton`). Use bible IDs so voice, colours and screen side stay consistent. | Name him ต้น (or define the new character in the bible first). | minor |
| 11 | Shot 5 | 4 s insert of the gold frames — static | OK length (≤6 s) but should carry a small push and be motivated by the line (temporal contiguity: show what is talked about while it is said — cognitive-science §4.2 item 5). | Cut the insert on/just after "แว่นอันนี้", with a slow push. | minor |

**What is good:** starting with a wide two-shot that establishes positions; giving the emotional line ("ยายใส่แว่นอันนี้มาสามสิบปี…") the close-up (GUIDE: "the most important line gets the closest shot"); the insert of the gold frames ties the scene to the episode's hero prop; 16:9 at 24 fps matches the series format.
