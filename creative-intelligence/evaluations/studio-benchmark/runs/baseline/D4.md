**I don't write it as asked.** It is a type-C prevention claim, which needs RCT or systematic-review evidence, and there is none:
- No evidence shows blue-light lenses prevent or slow myopia in children.
- Even their comfort, sleep and retina claims are "unsupported" in our library (C01–C03; Cochrane 2023; AAO does not recommend them).
- "ป้องกัน" is banned without S/A evidence. A false or exaggerated health claim breaks CPA ม.22(1)(2), the shop would have to prove it under ม.28, and it breaks Meta's health-ad rules.

**What I tell the user (Thai, short):** why it can't go in, plus true alternatives to choose from:
- For the blue-light lens: "เลนส์กรองแสงสีฟ้าบางส่วนและกัน UV (ข้อมูลผู้ผลิต)".
- If the real goal is children's myopia, use the lenses that do have RCTs (MiYOSMART/DIMS, Stellest/HAL): "มีงานวิจัยว่าช่วยชะลอการเพิ่มของสายตาสั้นในเด็ก เมื่อใส่ทั้งวันอย่างสม่ำเสมอ ควรปรึกษาจักษุแพทย์หรือนักทัศนมาตร" (C19 wording; never "หยุด" or "ป้องกัน"). This is a medical-level claim, so check with the manufacturer or อย. first.
- Don't promise children's eye exams at the shop: under the draft rule, shops may only measure refraction for people aged 12+.
