## What changed and why (one line each)
1. **"แว่นใหม่มาแล้ว" → named brand, model and colour:** a caption that names the product gives the reader something concrete and checkable. Names come only from `data/`.
2. **"สวยมากๆๆๆ" removed → one real detail:** empty praise carries no information. A specific feature from the maker data is what makes people stop (domain 08: a concrete image beats a slogan).
3. **"ราคาถูก" removed → a price line only if the user gives one:** "cheap" without a price is vague and can mislead, and the house rule says never invent a price or promo (ask each time).
4. **"คุณภาพดีที่สุดในโลก" removed:** an unprovable superlative is a false/exaggerated claim under the Consumer Protection Act ม.22(1). If challenged under ม.28 we must prove it. "ดีที่สุด" is also on the banned-word list (FACT_CHECK §6).
5. **"ใครไม่ซื้อถือว่าพลาด" removed:** confirmshaming / pressure FOMO is a manipulative tactic (dark-pattern list, domain 11 §11.2 step 2: customers who notice it feel tricked).
6. **"มาเลยๆ" → one clear CTA ("Come and try it on" + where to message):** a single concrete next step works better than a vague push (CONTENT_TYPES #8: one CTA).
7. **"ตลอด 24 ชม. ไม่มีวันหยุด" → real hours (Mon–Sat 09:30–18:00, taken from the 2026-11-23 notice; confirm Saturday hours with the user):** the shop closes on Sundays and the admin replies in opening hours, so "24 h, no holidays" is false (ม.22). The real contacts (LINE, phone) replace a promise we can't keep.
8. **"ค่ะ" register kept polite, "Time Optic" written as two words:** this is the house spelling for the shop name in running text.
9. **Hashtags #แว่น #ถูก #ดี → brand, place and product tags:** generic adjectives as tags reach nobody useful. Brand, location and model tags match what people actually search for and make the brand easier to remember.
10. **Language → English:** shop post captions follow the English-only house rule, with a Thai version offered only if the user asks for one.
