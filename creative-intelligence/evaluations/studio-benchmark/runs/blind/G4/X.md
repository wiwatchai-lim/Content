## What changed and why (one line each)

1. **"แว่นใหม่มาแล้ว" → named brand and model.** A specific product is searchable and believable; a vague "new glasses" gives no reason to stop (product-intro posts must say what it is).
2. **"สวยมากๆๆๆ" → concrete shape, material and colour from data.** Showing beats shouting. Repeated ๆๆๆ reads as spammy, and every fact comes from `data/`.
3. **"ราคาถูก" → the real price and what it includes, or nothing.** "Cheap" with no number is vague, and a price that hides the lens cost is drip pricing (dark pattern, CPA ม.22). The price is asked from the user every time.
4. **"คุณภาพดีที่สุดในโลก" → removed.** An unprovable superlative is a false/exaggerated claim under CPA ม.22(1) (the shop must prove it, ม.28). "ดีที่สุด" is also on the อย. banned-word list.
5. **"ใครไม่ซื้อถือว่าพลาด" → removed.** This is pressure/FOMO, a dark-pattern tone. Thai service politeness (เกรงใจ) works better with an invitation.
6. **"มาเลยๆ" → one soft, clear CTA ("ทักแชทถามรุ่นหรือจองเวลาลอง…นะคะ").** One action, polite particle นะคะ, matching the shop voice.
7. **"ทักแชทได้ตลอด 24 ชม. ไม่มีวันหยุด" → real hours, "ตอบในเวลาทำการ", closed Sunday.** The shop is closed on Sundays and staff don't answer 24/7, so the original promise is false (CPA ม.22).
8. **#แว่น #ถูก #ดี → brand, place and product hashtags.** Generic one-word tags bring no relevant audience; local and brand tags (#แว่นตาหัวหิน, #[แบรนด์]) match what buyers search.
9. **Added the location line.** Conversion content needs where and when; Hua Hin is the shop's real market.
10. **Added an English version.** House rule: posts and product captions are English-only (the Thai version is kept in case the user wants it for a Thai-only post).
