**What is good (keep):** The cover line "ดอกไม้ที่บานช้า ก็ยังเป็นดอกไม้" is a strong, simple metaphor that matches the channel name, so keep it as the spine of the piece. Mint's worry (friends with jobs, houses and travel while "เรายังอยู่ที่เดิม") is specific and relatable, and fits her role (worry and tiredness). The slide order cover → Mint → Tao → takeaway matches the channel's structure.

**Top 3 fixes:** (1) remove the logo and the promo slide; (2) fix Mint's costume and keep both characters faceless; (3) replace the fake Einstein quote and the shaming takeaway with Tao's own short, warm line.
