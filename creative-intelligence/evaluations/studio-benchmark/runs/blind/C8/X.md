# C8 — Songkran post concept, TIME OPTIC (TH+EN) (critique)

Critic: inline (not independent). This is an idea-stage concept, so there is no score (critic rule 12). Verdict: **fail**, because several [STOP] items in the cultural check fail.

**What it is:** a seasonal sale post that pairs sunglasses with Songkran water and Buddhist imagery, with a BOGO offer and a yellow ground.

| # | Location | Problem | Why | Fix | Severity |

The hero becomes the sunglasses with water: droplets beading on the lenses, a splash frozen behind the frame, bright midday sun. | critical |

**What is good / statements of meaning:** Tying sunglasses to Songkran is a natural fit: water, glare and sun in mid-April, and being outside all day. The water splash is the right visual seed, so keep it and move it onto the product. A seasonal hook timed to the holiday is good marketing practice (K1: check the calendar).

**Neutral questions for the maker:**
- Is the product polarised or hydrophobic-coated? If so, water on the lenses can carry a true product fact.
- Is the audience locals or tourists (A1–A2)? The answer changes the English register.
- Is there a real offer and date range from the user?

**Risks if posted as is:** legal exposure (ม.206), public backlash and boycott, and Meta ad rejection, from #1–#3 alone.
